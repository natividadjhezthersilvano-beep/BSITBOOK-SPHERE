Overview

Emerging technologies are new and developing technologies that improve how people live, work, communicate, and solve problems. Examples include Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Big Data Analytics, Internet of Things, Cloud Computing, and Cybersecurity.

This lesson also introduces important skills needed in the digital world, such as communication, creative problem-solving, critical thinking, and collaboration.

Modern technology and digital innovation

Artificial Intelligence (AI)

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that allows machines and computers to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. It helps machines recognize patterns, understand information, answer questions, and make decisions.

Artificial Intelligence and computer technology

Examples of AI

  • Typing Software: Identifies spelling and grammar mistakes and suggests words while typing.
  • Online Shopping: Recommends products based on previous searches, purchases, and user preferences.
  • Chatbots: Answer common questions and provide information through websites and online services.

Machine Learning

Machine Learning is a way of developing AI. It allows computers to learn from data and improve their performance without being programmed for every task.

For example, a computer can study thousands of pictures of cats and learn how to recognize cats in other images.

Machine learning and computer data processing

Methods of Machine Learning

  • Supervised Learning: Learns from labeled data and is used for predictions, such as detecting possible credit card fraud.
  • Unsupervised Learning: Finds patterns and groups similar information, such as identifying groups of customers.
  • Semi-supervised Learning: Uses both labeled and unlabeled data. It is useful when labeling all information is difficult or costly.
  • Reinforcement Learning: Learns through trial and error by choosing actions that provide better results or rewards.

Who Uses Machine Learning?

  • Financial Services: Detects fraud and analyzes financial information.
  • Government Agencies: Helps analyze information and improve public services.
  • Healthcare: Supports medical research and patient diagnosis.
  • Retail: Studies customer behavior and recommends products.
  • Oil and Gas: Helps find energy sources and predict equipment problems.

Deep Learning and Neural Networks

Deep Learning

Deep Learning is a type of Machine Learning inspired by the structure and functions of the human brain. It uses multiple layers of artificial neural networks to study information and recognize patterns.

Structure of an artificial neural network

Neural Networks

Neural Networks are computer systems designed to work similarly to the connections of neurons in the human brain. They learn by analyzing examples and identifying patterns.

For example, a neural network can study pictures of cars, houses, and other objects to learn how to recognize them.

Applications of Artificial Intelligence

Robotics and real-world AI applications

  • Robotics: Helps robots perform tasks such as assembling cars, packing products, cleaning homes, and assisting people.
  • Real-Time Translation: Translates text or speech from one language into another.
  • Digital Assistants: Assistants such as Alexa and Siri understand voice commands and answer questions.
  • Autonomous Systems: Perform tasks with little or no human involvement. Examples include self-driving vehicles, drones, and automated factory systems.
  • Gaming and Simulation: Augmented Reality (AR) and Virtual Reality (VR) are used in training, entertainment, shopping, and education.

AI in Business

  • Reasoning: Helps solve problems through logical decisions.
  • Knowledge: Helps analyze information for medical diagnosis, fraud prevention, and product recommendations.
  • Planning: Helps manage inventory, forecast demand, schedule activities, and improve transportation.
  • Communication: Uses language understanding and translation to make communication easier.

AI and Emerging Technologies in Healthcare

AI and other technologies help healthcare professionals understand health information, identify diseases, and improve patient care.

Medical Imaging and AI-Assisted Diagnosis

  • AI and Machine Learning: Help identify diseases, support diagnosis, and develop treatment plans.
  • Healthcare Robots: Assist with patient care, medical supplies, rehabilitation, and hospital cleaning.
  • Computer and Machine Vision: Help doctors examine medical images and scans.
  • Wearable Technology: Monitors heart rhythms, blood pressure, and physical activity.
  • AI-Enabled Genomics: Helps analyze genetic information to support personalized medical treatment.
  • 3D Printing: Helps create medical devices, surgical tools, and prosthetic limbs.
  • Digital Twins: Uses digital representations of patient information to help understand possible treatment outcomes.
  • 5G Technology: Supports faster transmission of medical files and remote healthcare services.
  • AI Neural Networks: Help analyze medical patterns and support diagnostic methods.

Big Data Analytics

Introduction to Data

Data refers to facts, numbers, observations, or information collected about people, objects, or events.

Database and DBMS

A database is an organized collection of information stored in a computer or on the cloud.

A Database Management System (DBMS) is software used to store, organize, manage, and retrieve information from databases.

What is Big Data Analytics?

Big Data Analytics is the process of examining large amounts of data to discover patterns, relationships, and useful information.

The process of Big Data Analytics

Steps in Big Data Analytics

  • Data Collection: Gathering information from websites, devices, transactions, and other sources.
  • Data Cleansing: Correcting or removing incorrect, duplicated, incomplete, or damaged information.
  • Data Exploration: Examining information to discover patterns and useful details.
  • Data Visualization: Presenting information through charts, graphs, and maps to make it easier to understand.

Applications of Big Data

  • Healthcare: Studies treatments, diseases, and medical information.
  • Media and Entertainment: Recommends content and improves advertising.
  • Traffic Optimization: Helps understand traffic and improve routes.
  • Flight Route Optimization: Helps airlines study passenger behavior and route performance.
  • E-Commerce: Recommends products and videos based on user activity.
  • Agriculture: Uses sensors and weather information to monitor crops and soil.

Internet of Things (IoT)

What is IoT?

The Internet of Things (IoT) refers to physical devices connected to the internet that collect, share, and exchange information.

Examples include smart lights, smartphones, security cameras, smart appliances, and wearable devices.

Stages of Internet of Things Architecture

Examples of IoT in Banking

  • Wearables: Smart watches and wearable devices can support contactless payments.
  • Connected Cars: Connected vehicles can support mobile banking services.
  • Banking at Home: Smart speakers can allow users to check balances and make payments through voice commands.

IoT Architecture

  1. Sensors and Actuators: Collect information and perform actions.
  2. Internet Gateways and Data Acquisition: Transfer and collect information through networks.
  3. Edge IT: Processes and analyzes data near the devices.
  4. Data Center and Cloud: Stores information and supports applications and services.

Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT)

The Industrial Internet of Things (IIoT) uses connected devices and smart machines in industries. It allows machines to collect, analyze, and share information in real time.

Examples of IIoT

  • A connected rat trap that sends a notification to a mobile phone.
  • A soil sensor that measures moisture and nutrients.
  • Smart parking and traffic management systems.
  • Automated automobile assembly lines.

Applications of IoT

  • Agriculture: Monitors soil, weather, irrigation, and crops.
  • Consumer Use: Smart homes and wearable devices improve convenience and connectivity.
  • Healthcare: Monitors patients and hospital equipment.
  • Insurance: Uses fitness tracking to support personalized policies.
  • Manufacturing: Tracks products and monitors equipment.
  • Retail: Helps manage warehouses and understand customer purchases.
  • Transportation: Uses GPS to improve routes and delivery services.
  • Utilities and Energy: Monitors energy usage and environmental conditions.

Cloud Computing

What is Cloud Computing?

Cloud Computing uses remote servers connected through the internet to store, manage, and access information instead of relying only on local storage.

Cloud Computing services and technology

Uses of Cloud Computing

  • Developing applications and online services.
  • Storing, backing up, and recovering data.
  • Hosting websites and blogs.
  • Providing software through the internet.
  • Analyzing data.
  • Streaming videos and audio.

Advantages of Cloud Computing

  • Backup and Recovery: Makes it easier to restore stored information.
  • Collaboration: Allows people to share files and work together.
  • Accessibility: Provides access to information from different locations.
  • Low Maintenance Cost: Reduces hardware and software maintenance costs.
  • Mobility: Allows users to access information through different devices.
  • Pay-per-use: Allows users to pay based on service usage.
  • Storage Capacity: Provides large online storage spaces.
  • Data Security: Provides security features to protect stored information.

Types of Cloud Computing

  • Public Cloud: A cloud service available to users through the internet and managed by a service provider.
  • Private Cloud: A cloud environment used by one organization.
  • Hybrid Cloud: Combines public and private cloud services.
  • Community Cloud: A cloud environment shared by organizations with similar needs.

Applications of Cloud Computing

  • Art Applications: Used for creating designs and images.
  • Business Applications: Supports business operations and communication.
  • Data Storage and Backup: Stores files and supports recovery.
  • Education Applications: Supports online learning and virtual classrooms.
  • Entertainment Applications: Supports online games and video conferencing.
  • Management Applications: Helps manage cloud resources and activities.
  • Social Applications: Supports online communication and social networking.

Cybersecurity and Cyber Attacks

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity refers to the technologies, processes, and controls used to protect computers, networks, programs, devices, and information from cyber attacks.

Cybersecurity and digital protection

Types of Cybersecurity

  • Critical Infrastructure Security: Protects important systems such as electricity grids, hospitals, water systems, and traffic lights.
  • Application Security: Protects software and applications using security measures such as antivirus software, firewalls, and encryption.
  • Network Security: Protects networks from unauthorized access and attacks.
  • Cloud Security: Protects information and resources stored in cloud environments.
  • IoT Security: Protects internet-connected devices such as smart appliances, routers, and security cameras.

What is a Cyber Attack?

A cyber attack is a deliberate attempt to access, damage, steal, or disrupt another person's or organization's computer system or information.

Types of Cyber Attacks

  • Malware: Malicious software that can damage systems or steal information.
  • Phishing: Fake emails or messages designed to steal personal information.
  • Man-in-the-Middle Attack: Secretly intercepts communication between two parties.
  • Denial-of-Service Attack: Overloads a system or network with traffic.
  • SQL Injection: Uses harmful SQL commands to access or manipulate database information.
  • Zero-Day Exploit: Takes advantage of a newly discovered security weakness.
  • Password Attack: Attempts to obtain or guess passwords.
  • Cross-Site Scripting: Injects harmful scripts into trusted websites.
  • Rootkits: Malicious tools that hide inside a system and may allow unauthorized control.
  • IoT Attacks: Target connected devices and their networks.

Tips to Avoid Cyber Attacks

  • Learn and practice cybersecurity principles.
  • Install and regularly update antivirus software.
  • Use a firewall for internet connections.
  • Install operating system and application updates.
  • Back up important information.
  • Control physical access to computers and network devices.
  • Secure Wi-Fi networks.
  • Use individual accounts for each user.
  • Limit access to sensitive information.
  • Regularly change passwords.

Communication Skills

What is Communication?

Communication is the process of sharing thoughts, information, emotions, and ideas from one person to another through speech, writing, gestures, symbols, and expressions.

The Communication Process

Process of Communication

  • Sender: The person who creates and sends the message.
  • Message: The information, idea, or feeling being shared.
  • Encoding: Changing the message into words, pictures, or symbols.
  • Medium: The method used to send the message, such as speaking or writing.
  • Decoding: Interpreting and understanding the received message.
  • Recipient: The person who receives the message.
  • Feedback: The response confirming that the message was received or understood.

Barriers to Communication

  • Physical barriers
  • Perceptual barriers
  • Emotional barriers
  • Cultural barriers
  • Language barriers
  • Gender barriers
  • Interpersonal barriers

Importance of Communication in the Workplace

Good communication helps employees understand instructions, ask questions, share information, and work with others. It improves teamwork, workplace efficiency, and professional relationships.

Important Communication Skills

  • Emotional Intelligence: Understanding and managing emotions.
  • Cohesion and Clarity: Sharing messages clearly and in an organized way.
  • Friendliness: Using a friendly and welcoming tone.
  • Confidence: Expressing ideas clearly.
  • Empathy: Understanding other people's feelings and opinions.
  • Respect: Valuing other people's ideas and differences.
  • Listening: Paying attention to what others are saying.
  • Open-mindedness: Being willing to consider different opinions.
  • Tone of Voice: Using an appropriate tone during conversations.
  • Asking Good Questions: Asking useful questions to improve understanding.

Creative Problem-Solving Skills

Creative problem-solving is the ability to think of different ways to solve a problem. It involves looking at a situation from different perspectives and considering possible solutions.

Creative Problem-Solving Process

Creativity in Problem-Solving

  • Creativity in Leadership: Using creative ideas to guide teams and solve challenges.
  • Encouraging Creativity: Allowing people to share ideas and explore solutions.
  • Creativity and Innovation: Developing new ideas, methods, and improvements.

Steps in Workplace Problem-Solving

  1. Define the Problem: Understand what needs to be solved.
  2. Generate Solutions: Think of possible ways to solve the problem.
  3. Evaluate Solutions: Compare the advantages and disadvantages of each solution.
  4. Implement the Solution: Choose and apply the most suitable solution.
  5. Assess the Solution: Check whether the solution worked.

Critical Thinking

Critical thinking is the ability to carefully examine, evaluate, and understand information before making a decision.

It involves questioning ideas, checking facts, recognizing personal biases, and considering different points of view.

Critical Thinking and Information Analysis

Applying Critical Thinking

  • Develop your own solution to an unexpected problem.
  • Compare advantages and disadvantages before deciding.
  • Avoid making assumptions about other people.
  • Recognize personal biases instead of accepting them without question.

Collaboration Skills

Collaboration skills are the abilities used when working with other people to complete a task, create something, or achieve a common goal.

Important Teamwork and Collaboration Skills

Types of Collaboration Skills

  • Communication Skills: Sharing ideas and information clearly with team members.
  • Emotional Intelligence: Understanding emotions and maintaining positive working relationships.
  • Respect for Diversity: Accepting different backgrounds, opinions, experiences, and ideas.

Key Takeaways

  • AI allows machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.
  • Machine Learning helps computers learn from data and improve their performance.
  • Deep Learning uses artificial neural networks to recognize patterns.
  • Big Data Analytics helps organizations understand large amounts of information.
  • IoT connects physical devices through the internet.
  • Cloud Computing provides online storage, applications, and services.
  • Cybersecurity protects systems, networks, devices, and information from cyber threats.
  • Communication, problem-solving, critical thinking, and collaboration are important digital workplace skills.
